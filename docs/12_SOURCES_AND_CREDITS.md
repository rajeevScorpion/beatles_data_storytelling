## The Beatles — official
### Albums / chronology
https://www.thebeatles.com/albums

Use:
release chronology and distinction between original catalogue and later releases.

### Please Please Me
https://www.thebeatles.com/please-please-me

Use:
early recording context; mixture of stage repertoire and Lennon–McCartney originals.

### Revolver
https://www.thebeatles.com/revolver

Use:
longer studio period, experimentation, and George Harrison having three compositions on the album.

### Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band
https://www.thebeatles.com/sgt-peppers-lonely-hearts-club-band-0

Use:
post-touring studio focus and extensive recording time.

### Abbey Road
https://www.thebeatles.com/abbey-road

Use:
official context identifying Abbey Road as the final Beatles album recorded, though not last released.

### Let It Be
https://www.thebeatles.com/let-it-be-0

Use:
final-release context.

### Past Masters official store listing
https://usastore.thebeatles.com/products/past-masters-volumes-1-2-2cd

Use:
track listing including the two German-language songs and writer credits.

### 2023 “Now And Then” / Red & Blue announcement
https://www.thebeatles.com/announcement

Use:
context for Anthology-era material and an optional future afterword extension. Do not add Now And Then to this dataset unless explicitly extending the source data.

# 01 — Project Brief

## Working title
# EIGHT YEARS THAT CHANGED THE SOUND
### THE BEATLES, 1962–1970
**A data story in 213 songs**

Alternate hero lockup:
> **EIGHT YEARS THAT CHANGED THE SOUND**  
> *How The Beatles moved from Beatlemania to the studio frontier — in 213 songs.*

Avoid the generic title “The Beatles: From Beatlemania to Abbey Road” as the primary heading. It can appear as descriptive body copy if useful.

## Objective
Build an immersive, single-page data story that explains the Beatles’ rapid creative evolution while allowing users to branch into albums, writers, singers, moods, themes, genres and lyrics.

The interface must feel like an editorial music experience, not a business-intelligence dashboard or discography database.

## Success criteria
A first-time visitor should leave understanding:
- the speed of change from 1962–1970;
- how covers fell away as original songwriting took over;
- the importance and changing shape of Lennon/McCartney authorship;
- George Harrison’s growing compositional presence;
- the shift from touring-band immediacy toward studio experimentation;
- the changing emotional/thematic landscape in the supplied lyrics metadata;
- how individual songs connect to eras, moods and themes.

A fan should be able to stay longer by exploring:
- songs by writer or lead singer;
- themes such as heartbreak, introspection and love;
- lyrics search across the corpus;
- genre evolution;
- related songs;
- in-page playback for a curated starter set.

## Single-page first
Prefer one long, choreographed page with:
- sticky narrative moments,
- modal/drawer song details,
- persistent exploration controls,
- one media player instance,
- progressive reveal,
- smooth transitions between visual eras.

Do not create separate routes unless a technical reason genuinely requires them. Even “Explore all songs” should be an overlay/drawer or an in-page mode.

## Tone
Curious, intelligent, musical, playful, historically grounded and emotionally resonant.

Do not assume the visitor already knows why *Revolver* matters or who wrote a given song. Explain without lecturing.

## Educational context
This is a classroom demonstration with no commercial intent. Still provide compact credits/backlinks for imagery, metadata and media sources. Do not imply that classroom purpose automatically makes every third-party asset freely reusable.
